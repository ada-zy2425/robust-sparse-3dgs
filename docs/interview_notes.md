# Interview notes

## Three-minute version

I built a reproducible sparse-view 3D Gaussian Splatting workflow for static industrial-style captures. The hard part was not invoking a trainer; it was preventing convincing but invalid results. I isolated held-out images, pinned upstream sources, added immutable receipts and an aggregate multi-GPU budget, and evaluated novel views after training-camera-only coordinate alignment.

Two technical decisions mattered. First, initialization receives a copied train-only directory because a COLMAP version canonicalized symlinks and broke the image-name/data-isolation contract. Second, I test resume against the same persisted prefix across a real densification boundary, including optimizer, strategy, RNG and held-out renders—not just whether a checkpoint loads.

The failed resume test became a root-cause exercise rather than a pass-by-tolerance change. I verified optimizer parameter binding, saved momentum/steps/LR/scheduler, strategy state, RNG, sample order and rebuilt Scene/Stage references. A continuous branch and two independent reloads were exact through the first forward pass; both reloads first differed during backward at roughly `5e-8`, and the first densification count still matched. I kept continuous execution as the formal-results boundary and instrumented later structural decisions to measure where the perturbation is amplified.

The most useful failure was sparse COLMAP registration. A frozen three-scene, 6/12/24-view matrix produced full coverage in only 1/9 COLMAP arms; the unsuccessful partial reconstructions stayed in the denominator. That converted a vague “3DGS quality problem” into a measurable capture/initialization reliability problem.

The first formal comparison produced a second concrete decision. A 100k-point VGGT initialization was worse than a 9.8k-point COLMAP initialization because the predicted cameras had much larger alignment residuals; this ruled out “more points should be better” and redirected the next control toward the official BA initialization path. That upstream switch also changes tracking, point construction/filtering and camera-model handling, so I reported it as a branch-level system comparison rather than assigning all gain to BA or poses. It gave full-coverage 24-view initializations on all three development scenes and improved all held-out metrics on two. The outdoor scene improved reliability and LPIPS but lost PSNR/SSIM and became larger/slower, so I report a scoped diagnosis rather than pretending BA is my algorithm or a universal win.

I then built the actually matched operation control. It saved the official-branch reconstruction immediately before BA, reloaded isolated copies and ran BA on only one. Mean reprojection error fell from 10.925 to 2.491 px on bonsai and 10.500 to 2.457 px on room. Under two downstream seeds, every pre-BA run failed the finite-state gate at step 100 while every post-BA run completed. That identifies a trainability effect for these snapshots, but because the failed arms have no valid held-out endpoints I do not claim a paired image-quality gain or separate camera-versus-point contributions.

I froze three unseen final scenes before scoring. The official BA branch completed all three at 24/24 coverage; the paired COLMAP runs reached only 20/24, 21/24 and 4/24 and were kept as partial-registration failures. That is useful external confirmation of the branch-level reliability policy, but it is not an isolated BA or conditional quality comparison because failed baselines have no protocol-valid score. The hardest bicycle result also shows the remaining fidelity gap: the object is recognizable, while foreground grass contains strong blur/floater artifacts.

For performance, I benchmarked every fixed held-out camera with CUDA synchronization, five warmups and 30 repeats per camera. Across eight development checkpoints, the results made Gaussian count and scene-dependent model growth visible instead of quoting a cherry-picked camera. A separate 3k schedule cut optimization time by 60.8% and PLY size by 65.6% versus 7k, but LPIPS regressed, so it remains a product-facing quality/cost knob.

## Claims discipline

Say what is measured: task isolation, complete failure accounting, three development plus three frozen final scenes, matched BA-operation trainability evidence on two development snapshots, artifact export, multi-camera performance, a real interactive viewer check and bounded execution. Do not claim robust resume, commercial VGGT rights, geometry accuracy, a paired BA quality gain, strong statistical generalization, a novel BA method or custom CUDA work.
